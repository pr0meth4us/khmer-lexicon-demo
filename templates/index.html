Known limitations
  • 24 entries have no Khmer form at all
  • 1,657 entries have no English gloss — they came from Khmer-only documents and display as —
  • 21 entries have a Khmer field containing no Khmer characters: legacy-font OCR read as Latin, plus six French grammar terms filed in the wrong column. Excluded from the automaton.
  • 121 exactly duplicated Khmer forms, and 164 near-duplicates one grapheme cluster apart
  • Coverage is terminology, not vocabulary: ក្រសួង ("ministry") is not an entry, so it is not highlighted
